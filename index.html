Data Center Cooling LLM


The problem: Data centers use enormous amounts of water to keep their servers cool, and the AI boom is only increasing the demand.


What I built: A research loop that turns a local LLM into a cooling-systems inventor. It learns how real-world data center cooling systems and prototypes work and what the water-shortage problem looks like, then keeps generating and stress-testing ideas until it lands on a water-free cooling approach that doesn't exist yet.


The constraint: No cloud GPUs. It runs fully on my MacBook Air with 8 GB RAM using Qwen2.5 7B, pushed to its maximum output and reasoning, across multiple full-night sessions.

Aug 2026MV3

Ontario Parks Multi-Park & Site Helper



What it is: A Manifest V3 Chrome extension designed to eliminate the frustration of booking high-demand campsites.


How to use it: Open the Ontario Parks reservation page for your dates, click the green button and leave the tab open. The extension handles the rest.


What it does: It runs silently in the background, parsing the site's DOM in real time. If someone cancels or a spot opens up, it instantly plays a custom audio alert and adds the site directly to your cart, so you can secure the booking before anyone else.
